Privacy

Last updated 20 September 2026. Leone Awakens Studio is Angelo Leone, working alone from Italy. Written in plain words on purpose: if something below is not true of this site, it is a mistake and I want to hear about it.

This website collects nothing

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel and no cookie. Nothing is stored in your browser. The fonts are served from this same site, not from a font company, so no third party learns that you were here. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub records ordinary server logs (including IP addresses) for its own security and abuse handling; that is GitHub's processing, not mine, and it is described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

The clips on the page are served from this site. The YouTube section is different and it is marked: the YouTube player is created only when you click a clip, and from that moment YouTube sees you as it would on its own site. If you never click, it never loads.

If you write to me

The only way this site can reach me is email. If you send one, I keep it in my mailbox for as long as the conversation and the work it leads to are alive, plus whatever an invoice obliges me to keep. I use it to answer you and to do the job. I do not put it on a mailing list, I do not sell it and I do not pass it to anyone who is not doing the work with me. Ask me to delete it and I will, unless a tax rule says the invoice has to stay.

The TikTok and YouTube tools

I run software that publishes my own videos to my own YouTube and TikTok channels. It acts on my accounts only, with my own authorisation, and it does not read, store or process anybody else's personal data. If that software is ever authorised against another person's account, it can only do what that person explicitly allows when they grant access, and it holds nothing beyond the token needed to publish, which they can revoke at any time from their own account settings.

Where things are

Files, correspondence and project material live on machines in Italy and in the accounts of the services named above. Nothing about visitors is exported anywhere, because nothing about visitors is collected.

Your rights, and how to use them

Under the GDPR you can ask what I hold about you, ask for a copy, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and complain to the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. In practice what I hold about you is the emails you sent me. Write to wangelowdwd@gmail.com and say what you want done.
