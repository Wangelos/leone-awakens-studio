    Who makes it

    Angelo Leone, founder. Based in Italy, working in English.

    The studio runs its own pipeline rather than an off-the-shelf editor: Whisper for the transcription, cuts snapped to whole sentences, captions timed word by word, three.js for the 3D and Remotion for the motion graphics.

    The habit underneath all of it is measurement. A loop is not called seamless until a detector has compared its last frame to its first. A sound effect is not called placed until its arrival has been read back off the finished render. It is slower, and it is the reason nothing here needs a disclaimer.

    The Leone Awakens channel is where all of it is tested in public before it is sold.
