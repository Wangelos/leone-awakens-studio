About

Angelo Leone — founder. Based in Italy, working in English.

The studio runs its own pipeline rather than an off-the-shelf editor: Whisper for transcription, cuts snapped to whole sentences, captions timed word by word, three.js for the 3D and Remotion for the motion graphics.

Sound design is measured, not guessed. Every effect is placed against the mix and then verified against the finished render, so what was planned is what you hear.

The Leone Awakens channel is the laboratory. Formats, animations and the audio system are tested there first, and only what survives becomes a service.
